// About

Hey! I'm Ibrahim...

I'm a creative developer and technical artist with a background in game programming, 3D modelling, animation, and VFX compositing. I'm proficient in C++ and C#, with hands-on experience building 2D and 3D interactive experiences in Unreal Engine and Unity — and I like going one level deeper, which is why I built my own engine from the ground up rather than only working on top of someone else's.

I'm driven by game history, mechanics, and collaborative problem-solving. Whether it's writing a custom graphics pipeline, shipping a museum installation, or prototyping a game jam idea in a week, I care about understanding how the thing actually works.
