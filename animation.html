A Different Timeline

Before LaSalle, I spent two years at Vancouver Film School — a Foundation year in Visual Arts & Design, then a diploma in 3D Design & Visual Effects. That's where I learned to light a scene in Maya and Redshift, composite in Foundry Nuke, and cut and finish motion work in After Effects and Illustrator. It's a different set of tools than my engine work, but the same instinct — figure out how the thing is actually made.
